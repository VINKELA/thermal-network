2 · Annual energy and exploratory input ranges

Annual heat starts from Canadian total-energy benchmarks, end-use shares and conversion factors. Shape runs impose equal annual energy; physical sensitivity runs calculate space-heating energy independently.

Useful heat = area × final-energy intensity × end-use share × conversion. The 0.85 conversion defaults are assumptions; values above 1 represent an assumed seasonal heat-pump COP. Published residential shares are from 2019 and commercial shares from 2017; they are sector proxies, not local measurements.
